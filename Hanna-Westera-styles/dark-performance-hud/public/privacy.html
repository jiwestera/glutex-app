Privacy Policy — Glutex

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Glutex ("the app") is a workout planning and tracking app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what choices you have.

The short version

By default, everything you do in Glutex — your workout logs, custom exercises, splits, and preferences — is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use one of two optional features: Cross-Device Sync or the AI Coach.

Information stored locally on your device

Workout logs, personal records, custom exercises and splits, warmup routines, and app preferences (units, theme, accent color) are saved in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you enable Cross-Device Sync below. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Cross-Device Sync (optional)

If you choose to create an account and sign in, the app collects and stores:

  • Your email address (used only to identify your account)
  • Your workout logs, custom splits, custom exercises, warmups, and related training data

This data is stored using Firebase (a Google Cloud service) and is used solely to make your data available across your own devices. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than providing the sync feature to you. Access is restricted so that only your signed-in account can read or write your own data.

If you never sign in, none of this applies to you — the app works fully offline and locally.

AI Coach

When you ask the AI Coach a question or generate a custom routine, the text of your question (and general context like your training frequency and unit preference — never your name, email, or workout history) is sent to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This text is not stored by us beyond what's needed to process the request and is not linked to your identity.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers
  • We don't use analytics services to track your behavior
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing

Third-party services we rely on

  • Firebase (Google) — account sign-in and optional data sync
  • Google Gemini API — generates AI Coach responses
  • Render — hosts the server that relays AI Coach requests

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data in transit.

Your choices

  • Cross-Device Sync is entirely optional — the app is fully functional without it
  • You can sign out of sync at any time from the app's Options panel
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data
  • To request deletion of a synced account and its cloud-stored data, contact us below

Children's privacy

Glutex is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
